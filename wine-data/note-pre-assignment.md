## Feature importance — preliminary assessment

### Strong predictors (clear separation across quality classes)
- alcohol — median rises steadily from quality 5 to 9
- volatile acidity — decreases with quality (acetic acid = vinegar taste)
- sulphates — higher in better wines (antimicrobial, preservative)

### Moderate predictors
- color — red/white have different chemical profiles
- citric acid — freshness, higher in quality wines
- density — correlated with alcohol and sugar

### Weak / uncertain predictors
- residual sugar — high variance, no clear quality trend
- free sulfur dioxide — protective but not quality-defining
- total sulfur dioxide — correlated with free SO2
- pH — minor role
- chlorides — minor role
- fixed acidity — minor role
- RestECG — not applicable (hearts dataset column)
